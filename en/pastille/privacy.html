Privacy Policy – Pastille

Effective date: 2026-11-09

This policy was drafted in French. This English translation is provided for convenience; the French version prevails in case of discrepancy.

Publisher: 9575-4255 Québec inc., doing business as Solutions Passerelle TI, business corporation (NEQ 1182529777) – Québec (Québec), Canada

Person in charge of the protection of personal information: the President of the company – SPTI@solutionspasserelleti.ca

This policy is established in accordance with Québec's Act respecting the protection of personal information in the private sector (CQLR, c. P-39.1) and, where applicable, the Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (S.C. 2000, c. 5).

Summary

Pastille runs entirely on your computer. Your copies, your anonymization dictionary and your settings stay on your device, encrypted. Pastille sends the publisher no information about you, and the publisher sells none. There are no ads, no in-app usage analytics and no account to create. Only the information you choose to send us (an email, for example) and the information Microsoft provides to us are described below.

Information processed on your device

To work, Pastille processes locally:

  • the content you copy (text, and the paths of copied files; the files themselves are not duplicated);
  • the images you copy (screenshots, images copied from a browser, etc.): a screenshot that Windows has already saved in your Pictures › Screenshots folder is not duplicated, Pastille keeps only a link to it; other images are saved, encrypted, in the app's folder;
  • the name of the app a copy comes from, only to apply the "excluded apps" list;
  • the title of the last window you used, shown to indicate where the "Paste" button will paste (it is not saved);
  • your anonymization dictionary (original values and their replacements);
  • your settings (languages, display options, excluded apps, etc.);
  • a technical error log, unencrypted, which does not contain the content of your copies but may contain the path of a file involved.

This data, except the error log, is stored in the app's folder on your device, encrypted with Windows Data Protection (DPAPI): it can only be read from your Windows account (or by a program running under that account). It is never sent to the publisher.

Payment card numbers: a copied text that contains one is never written to disk; it stays in memory until Pastille closes. An image is not analyzed: it is saved, encrypted, like other images.

A notice shown at first launch, before anything is saved, explains what Pastille records.

Pastille uses no technology that allows you to be identified, located or profiled.

Default privacy settings

Without any action on your part:

  • the history is encrypted;
  • copies made from the main password managers (excluded apps), and those the source app marks as confidential, are never saved;
  • payment card numbers copied as text are never written to disk;
  • the window does not show up on every copy;
  • Pastille windows are hidden from screen sharing and screenshots (Teams, Zoom, etc.); this does not protect against a photo of the screen;
  • Shield mode (which transforms every copied text) is off until you turn it on.

You can change these settings, and pause recording at any time from the tray icon menu.

What Windows keeps on its side

Independently of Pastille, Windows may keep what you copy in its own clipboard history (Win+V) and sync it across your devices, depending on your Windows settings (Settings › System › Clipboard). Pastille asks Windows not to keep the texts it restores to real values, but Pastille cannot remove from the Windows history what other apps placed there. Likewise, the Windows Snipping Tool may save your screenshots in your Pictures › Screenshots folder, depending on its own settings; those files are yours, and Pastille never modifies or deletes them.

Windows and Microsoft Store features

  • Purchase, free trial and license of Pastille Pro: handled by the Microsoft Store (the trial is a free Store add-on whose acquisition date Microsoft records). Microsoft processes the necessary information (Microsoft account, payment), possibly outside Québec, under its own privacy statement: https://privacy.microsoft.com. The publisher does not receive your payment information.
  • Spell checker: Pastille uses the spell checker built into Windows, which works locally.
  • Reading text from images ("Read text" button): Pastille uses the character recognition built into Windows, which works locally; the image is not sent anywhere.
  • Updates: distributed by the Microsoft Store.

Information the publisher may receive

  • If you write to us (email, support): we use your address and the content of your message only to reply to you, and we keep them for as long as needed to handle your request, then for at most two years.
  • Data provided by Microsoft: through its Partner Center, Microsoft provides us with aggregate, non-identifying statistics (downloads, purchases, breakdown by country, age group and device) and the reviews published on the Store listing. Microsoft does not give us your name, your email address or your payment information.
  • Crash reports: if Pastille crashes, Windows may send an error report to Microsoft, depending on your Windows diagnostic settings (Settings › Privacy & security › Diagnostics & feedback). Microsoft shows us a summary (number of crashes, where in the code); on preview versions of Windows (Windows Insider), the report may contain a copy of the app's memory at the time of the crash, which may include a copy in progress. We use these reports only to fix errors.

Third-party services you choose to use

If you paste text into another service (search engine, artificial intelligence tool, messaging app, etc.), that text is subject to that service's rules. Shield mode and anonymization help you remove personal information from a text before pasting it (images are not masked), but this is reversible masking (the real values stay in your dictionary), not anonymization within the meaning of the law, and automatic detection does not recognize everything (in particular addresses, dates of birth and medical information): review the text before sending it.

Retention and deletion

You stay in control of your data:

  • delete a copy with 🗑, or the whole history with "Clear all";
  • Pastille flags copies older than the number of days you choose and offers to delete them in one go;
  • the free edition keeps at most 100 text copies, 10 images, 5 pinned copies and 3 anonymization rules, and does not record file copies; Pastille Pro keeps up to 2000 copies (texts and files) and 200 images;
  • an image encrypted by Pastille is erased when it leaves the history; for a linked screenshot or a file copy, only the link is removed, never the file itself;
  • when the Pastille Pro trial ends (or after a refund), you have 7 days to buy Pastille Pro; during that period, you may choose which anonymization rules to delete and confirm this cleanup; upon that confirmation or, failing it, when the period ends, the oldest copies, images and pinned copies beyond the free edition's limits, the excess rules and the links to files are removed;
  • anonymization rules created by automatic detection are deleted automatically after 30 days; the ones you create yourself stay until you delete them;
  • exporting the dictionary produces an unencrypted file, under your responsibility (Pastille warns you before the export);
  • an unreadable history set aside (damaged file, other Windows account) is deleted after 30 days;
  • uninstalling Pastille removes its data from the device.

Your rights and remedies

Pastille's data remains under your sole control, on your device: the publisher has no access to it. For the information the publisher holds (your emails, for example), you may request access, correction or deletion; we reply within 30 days. For any question or complaint about the protection of personal information, write to the person in charge of the protection of personal information at the address above.

You may also contact the Commission d'accès à l'information du Québec (www.cai.gouv.qc.ca) or the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada (www.priv.gc.ca).

Children

Pastille is not specifically aimed at children and collects no personal information, including from persons under 14.

Changes

This policy may be updated, for example when new features are added. The effective date will then be changed, and any significant change will be announced in the app or on the Microsoft Store listing.
