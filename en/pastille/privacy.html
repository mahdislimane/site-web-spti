Privacy Policy – Pastille

Effective date: 2026-11-09

This policy was drafted in French. This English translation is provided for convenience; the French version prevails in case of discrepancy.

Publisher: 9575-4255 Québec inc., doing business as Solutions Passerelle TI, business corporation (NEQ 1182529777) – Québec (Québec), Canada

Person in charge of the protection of personal information: the President of the company – SPTI@solutionspasserelleti.ca

This policy is established in accordance with Québec's Act respecting the protection of personal information in the private sector (CQLR, c. P-39.1) and, where applicable, the Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (S.C. 2000, c. 5).

Summary

Pastille runs entirely on your computer. Your copies, your anonymization dictionary and your settings stay on your device, encrypted. The publisher does not collect, receive, disclose or sell any personal information about you. There are no ads, no usage analytics and no account to create.

Information processed on your device

To work, Pastille processes locally:

  • the content you copy (text, and the paths of copied files; the files themselves are not duplicated);
  • the name of the app a copy comes from, only to apply the "excluded apps" list;
  • your anonymization dictionary (original values and their replacements);
  • your settings (languages, display options, excluded apps, etc.);
  • a technical error log, which does not contain the content of your copies.

This data is stored in the app's folder on your device, encrypted with Windows Data Protection (DPAPI): it can only be read from your Windows account (or by a program running under that account). It is never sent to the publisher.

Payment card numbers: a copy that contains one is never written to disk; it stays in memory until Pastille closes.

A notice shown at first launch, before anything is saved, explains what Pastille records.

Pastille uses no technology that allows you to be identified, located or profiled.

Default privacy settings

Without any action on your part:

  • the history is encrypted;
  • copies made from the main password managers (excluded apps), and those the source app marks as confidential, are never saved;
  • payment card numbers are never written to disk;
  • the window does not show up on every copy;
  • Pastille windows are hidden from screen sharing and screenshots (Teams, Zoom, etc.); this does not protect against a photo of the screen;
  • Shield mode (which transforms every copied text) is off until you turn it on.

You can change these settings, and pause recording at any time from the tray icon menu.

What Windows keeps on its side

Independently of Pastille, Windows may keep what you copy in its own clipboard history (Win+V) and sync it across your devices, depending on your Windows settings (Settings › System › Clipboard). Pastille asks Windows not to keep the texts it restores to real values, but it cannot remove from the Windows history what other apps placed there.

What goes through Microsoft

  • Purchase, free trial and license of Pastille Pro: handled by the Microsoft Store (the trial is a free Store add-on whose acquisition date Microsoft records). Microsoft processes the necessary information (Microsoft account, payment), possibly outside Québec, under its own privacy statement: https://privacy.microsoft.com. The publisher does not receive your payment information.
  • Spell checker: Pastille uses the spell checker built into Windows, which works locally.
  • Updates: distributed by the Microsoft Store.

Third-party services you choose to use

If you paste text into another service (search engine, artificial intelligence tool, messaging app, etc.), that text is subject to that service's rules. Shield mode and anonymization help you remove personal information before pasting, but this is reversible masking (the real values stay in your dictionary), not anonymization within the meaning of the law, and automatic detection does not recognize everything (in particular addresses, dates of birth and medical information): review the text before sending it.

Retention and deletion

You stay in control of your data:

  • delete a copy with 🗑, or the whole history with "Clear all";
  • Pastille flags copies older than the number of days you choose and offers to delete them in one go;
  • the free edition keeps at most 100 text copies, 5 pinned copies and 3 anonymization rules, and does not record file copies (for those, only the link is removed from the history, never the file itself); when the Pastille Pro trial ends (or after a refund), you have 7 days to choose what to keep, after which the oldest items beyond these limits are deleted;
  • anonymization rules created by automatic detection are deleted automatically after 30 days; the ones you create yourself stay until you delete them;
  • exporting the dictionary produces an unencrypted file, under your responsibility (Pastille warns you before the export);
  • an unreadable history set aside (damaged file, other Windows account) is deleted after 30 days;
  • uninstalling Pastille removes its data from the device.

Your rights and remedies

As the publisher holds no personal information about you, there is no information for it to disclose, correct or delete; your data remains under your sole control, on your device. For any question or complaint about the protection of personal information, write to the person in charge of the protection of personal information at the address above.

You may also contact the Commission d'accès à l'information du Québec (www.cai.gouv.qc.ca) or the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada (www.priv.gc.ca).

Children

Pastille is not specifically aimed at children and collects no personal information, including from persons under 14.

Changes

This policy may be updated, for example when new features are added. The effective date will then be changed, and any significant change will be announced in the app or on the Microsoft Store listing.
