Pastille

Clipboard manager for Windows

Pastille keeps a history of what you copy, pastes it back into the previous window and helps you mask personal information before sending it elsewhere.

Coming soon to the Microsoft Store

Copy history

Find text you copied an hour or a week ago, and pin the ones you reuse often.

One-click paste

One click pastes the copy into the window you were working in. Shortcut Win+Shift+V.

Anonymization

A dictionary replaces names, emails, phone numbers or file numbers with codes, then restores them on the way back.

Shield mode

Copy a text, paste it into an AI tool without your personal information, and get the answer back with the real values. Everything happens on your computer.

Spell checker

Mistakes are underlined in your copies, with suggestions on right-click.

Private by default

Encrypted history on your computer, password managers excluded, windows hidden during screen sharing.
